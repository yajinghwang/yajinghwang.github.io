I'm a PhD candicate at the [Department of Communication and Culture, BI Norwegian Business](https://www.bi.no/en/research/find-department/department-of-communication-and-culture/), advised by [Prof.Dr. Christoph Lutz](https://www.bi.no/en/about-bi/employees/department-of-communication-and-culture/christoph-lutz/). My PhD is part of the interdisciplinary project [Evidence-informed and human-centered data governance policies based on privacy-enhancing DEsign and CODE (DECODE)](https://futuresociety.ch/decode.github.io), funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation. Prior to my PhD, I obtained a Research MSc in Communication Sciecne at University of Amsterdam, advised by [Dr. S.R. (Sindy) Sumter](https://www.uva.nl/en/profile/s/u/s.r.sumter/s.r.sumter.html), and a BA in Journalism and Communication & International Relations and Orgnizations at Shanghai International Studies University, with exchange studies at the University of Oxford and Korea University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **human-computer interaction(HCI), research through design (RtD), and human-centered data governance**. My PhD research examines how deisgn and code can make data processing and AI-mediated inference in smart environments understandable and actionable, thereby supporting meaningful privacy agency and human-centered data governance. My work combines **empirical and design-oriented approaches** to study users' cognitive processing and behavior change mechanism and how choice archetecture and interface deisgn can support privacy agency.
