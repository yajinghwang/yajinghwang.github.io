## Course Overview

Students will study theories of social practices and systems thinking, building on the knowledge that was acquired in the Foundations and Building Blocks courses. Students will further deepen their knowledge of qualitative and quantitative research methods and acquire skills of web scraping and machine learning.

A major part of the semester is organized around a group project (theme: health or mobility).
Students will work in small project groups to address a real-world problem identified by a CSSci
Challenge Partner. In the course of this project, students will:

- Design a viable research question that could be approached by the means of qualitative and
quantitative research methods;
- Compile a dataset from different sources, e.g. Challenge Partner’s data, web scraping, statistical records, and other open-data sources;
- Apply ethnographic, machine learning, and natural language processing techniques to answer
the group project research question;
- Create professional presentations of the project and its deliverables;
- Present the intervention plan to the Challenge Partner and the CSSci community.
