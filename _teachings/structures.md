## Course Overview

This course will cover structural cleavages and inequalities in society. Students will learn how structural inequalities may translate into ‘biased’ algorithic decision-making and Artificial Intelligence (AI), and you are challenged to propose interventions that result in less biased algroithms. The learning trajectories are:

- Predictive analytics: create a future scenario plan
- Ethical data science: machine learning code portfolio
- Policy paper: analysis of inequalities from a critical perspective
- Prototype web aplication: demonstrate how ethical principles apply to the real-world.
